# Overview

Risks hide in spreadsheets and side chats. This demo walks through adding risks on a project, editing them as facts change, and keeping mitigation visible to the team.

## What Risk Management covers
time: 45-60s
video: risk-demo-00-overview.mp4

- Why project risks belong in Sitetracker, not offline lists
- Core fields: description, category, score, owner, status
- How risks relate to the project lifecycle

# Add risks

Create risks as soon as they are identified so owners and leadership see them early.

## Add a new risk
time: 60-90s
video: risk-demo-01-add-risk.mp4

- Open Risk Management from a project
- Create a risk with title, description, and category
- Assign an owner and initial likelihood / impact

## Score and prioritize
time: 45-75s
video: risk-demo-02-score-prioritize.mp4

- Set likelihood and impact to drive risk score
- Sort or filter to surface the highest risks
- Patterns for intake during kickoff and weekly reviews

# Edit and manage

Risks change. Keep the record honest as mitigation progresses or exposure grows.

## Edit an existing risk
time: 60-90s
video: risk-demo-03-edit-risk.mp4

- Update description, owner, and scores inline
- Change status as the risk moves through its lifecycle
- Leave a clear trail for auditors and hand-offs

## Capture mitigation and next steps
time: 45-75s
video: risk-demo-04-mitigation.mp4

- Document mitigation actions against the risk
- Tie follow-ups to owners and target dates
- Close or accept risks when residual exposure is clear

# Review and report

Use the risk list for program reviews and customer conversations.

## Filter, sort, and review the risk list
time: 45-60s
video: risk-demo-05-review-list.mp4

- Filter by status, owner, or score band
- Prepare a concise view for steering / ops meetings
- Export or share when leadership needs a snapshot
